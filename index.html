Magnusson Box Generator

Custom inserts for the Magnusson compartment organiser (Screwfix 850JC).

Click a box to edit it. Shift-click or drag to select a block, Cmd/Ctrl-click to add. Join turns the selected cells in a column into one box (1–3 cells long); double-click does the same.
Box shape
Front cell first, comma separated. Standard cell is 66.5 × 64.5; the cell in front of the handle is 51.5 long.
Front is the handle side of the case. Standard is the 6.74 mm chamfer with R9 blends from the v7 box. Handle notch: a step cut across the front, with a 45° lead-in. Its across size runs from the corner, and along is its depth.
Compartments
Compartments don't have to follow the case cells. For example, a 2-cell box can be split into 3. Sizes are relative, front to back and left to right, and they override the count.
Name tag
Simple stroke font: A–Z, 0–9 and - + . , : / # ( ) % &
The tray rests on the box's tapered lower walls. Print it in clear filament to see what's underneath without lifting it.
